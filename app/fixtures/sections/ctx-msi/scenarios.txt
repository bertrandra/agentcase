Compare the AS-IS baseline against isolated and combined levers: AI automation alone, location arbitrage alone, a phased service-center transformation (X% relocation to France per year), and the combined plays. All scenarios share the same team, scope, ramp and token assumptions — only the levers differ. Note: if your team is currently nearshore/offshore, relocating to France raises the day-rate — the France scenarios then show a cost increase (sovereignty premium), which is the intended reality.
