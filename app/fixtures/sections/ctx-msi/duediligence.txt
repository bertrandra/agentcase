This register lists what a client must confirm for the model to be defensible — not what the model computes. It deliberately surfaces 0 coefficients that are hardcoded in the calculation path and editable nowhere in the interface. They materially drive the euro result, so until now the question “where does that number come from?” had no answer, even internally. Surfacing them is not a criticism of the model — every model has coefficients; the defect was that they were unseeable.
Where these values come from. AgentCase ships with defaults for the metric anchors and for the model’s internal coefficients. These are engineering estimates: no study, dataset or delivery record is cited for them, and they are not benchmarks. That is what this register is for — each becomes a confirmed input once validated against the client’s own records. Rows marked PC4 unvalidated and PC5 embedded are the ones still awaiting that confirmation.
