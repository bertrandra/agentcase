1 · Vision & Engagement
Agentic Software Engineering Reference Model	AgentCase = the quantified Economics part (Part 6) of the merged model	The target unified model that fuses the referential playbook (People, Process, Platform, Governance) with AgentCase economics. AgentCase supplies Part 6 — abaques, glass ceiling, vFTE, ROI — while the referential supplies the operating and delivery model.	Mapped in AgentCase
Agentic Software Factory	The delivery system AgentCase quantifies economically	Capgemini Software Engineering France ambition for 2026: not to add AI to existing practice but to rethink delivery around human-agent orchestration, under the assumed "necessary forward imbalance" posture. AgentCase turns that ambition into a defensible business case.	Aligned
L1 — Ad-hoc Assistance (AI assists individuals)	Adoption ramp 0-25% - A0/A1 authorization - vFTE < 10%	Individual chat-based AI use with no shared rules or structured workflow; delivery stays manual at its core. Indicative project-price gain 0-10% (genAI). In AgentCase this is the low end of the adoption ramp with suggestion-only authorization.	Mapped in AgentCase
L2 — Targeted Automation (AI assists team activities)	Adoption ramp 25-55% - A2 - vFTE 10-25%	Targeted task automation (code, tests, docs, quality, security) via project rules; the human stays dominant for orchestration and validation. Indicative gain 10-25% (genAI). Most 2026 market deployments sit at L2-L3.	Mapped in AgentCase
L3 — Augmented Delivery (AI does, human monitors)	Adoption ramp 55-80% - A3 - vFTE 25-45%	Agents embedded in CI/CD and connected to the tooling; the team supervises, validates and arbitrates exceptions with risk-based review effort. Indicative gain 25-50% (agentic). This is the first level where gains are genuinely engageable.	Mapped in AgentCase
L4 — Autonomous Organization (AI orchestrates, human governs)	Adoption ramp > 80% - A4 - vFTE > 45%	Multi-agent coordination and continuous delivery at scale; humans focus on governance, prioritisation and business alignment. Indicative gain 50-80% (agentic). L4 may be shown as a target but is not yet contractually engaged.	Mapped in AgentCase
Engagement context	Engagement context selector (MSC / MSI / STA)	The transversal reading grid of the whole referential: the three contexts differ by our capacity to control the environment (capacite de maitrise), which conditions every lever — reachable maturity, engageable gains, tooling, data and measurement.	Mapped in AgentCase
Forfait on Capgemini infrastructure	MSC context — full control	Fixed-price on Capgemini infrastructure: complete control of the agentic stack, tooling, data, context, CI/CD and gain measurement (in-house protocol). The highest maturity level is engageable here. AgentCase models this as the MSC coefficient set (neutral baseline).	Mapped in AgentCase
Forfait on client infrastructure	MSI context — partial control	Fixed-price on client infrastructure: we adapt to the client stack and can propose transferable assets; data and CI/CD are shared under client rules; gain measurement is negotiated. Intermediate engageable maturity. AgentCase MSI coefficients derate ramp, ceiling and billable setup.	Mapped in AgentCase
Assistance technique (staff augmentation)	STA context — weakest control	Technical assistance: tools imposed by the client, tightly framed data, endured process, difficult measurement, limited asset reuse. The most constrained engageable maturity. AgentCase STA coefficients apply the strongest deratings and widest uncertainty.	Mapped in AgentCase
Project eligibility (gate x potential)	Eligibility & Abaques tab (EV-15): go / conditional / no-go	Now implemented as a gate: six prerequisites (agent-accessible platform via API/MCP, mastered tech and standard architecture, clear stable specs, scope < 500k LoC, collaborative client, qualified resources) plus red-flag legacy screening and a delivery-nature flag (standard/embedded/critical/sovereign) that hardens the gate. A > 500k LoC or an unmet critical prerequisite forces NO-GO (may stay eligible per task). The verdict annotates Decision Intelligence.	Mapped in AgentCase
Internal capability build (Volet A)	Program tab (EV-22): 50 -> 200 -> 500 ramp	The internal Capgemini transformation, distinct from the client engagement (Volet B): capacity milestones (illustratively 50 operational -> 200 in training -> 500 targeted), carried by the champion + preceptor network. Time-to-mass is driven by preceptor ratio and training throughput.	Mapped in AgentCase
Maturity transition criteria	Program tab (EV-14): L->L objective gates	Maturity rises on evidence, not ambition. Objective gates: L2->L3 requires agent-recommendation acceptance >=90%, human override 5-15%, 0 P1 incident caused by an agent, all gates green for 90 days and Design Authority approval; L3->L4 requires stable multi-agent orchestration, measured value at scale and AI Office + Design Authority sign-off.	Mapped in AgentCase
Product profile (EV-13, unified pillars)	Eligibility & Abaques + Product status (v14.8): one profile, one coefficient matrix	v14.8 unifies the use-case pillar and the product status into ONE product profile with EV-13 semantics: Greenfield, Feature add, Modernisation, Maintenance (End-of-life maps to Maintenance; Critical is NOT a profile — it lives only in the eligibility delivery-nature flag). One coefficient per profile x phase (editable in Configure) drives BOTH the effort weighting and the Eligibility potential matrix, displayed as Strong/Medium/Cautious with the coefficient in parentheses.	Mapped in AgentCase
2 · Process & SDLC
SDLC (logical activity set)	11 lifecycle phases (L0 -> L10)	A logical set of activities, not a fixed sequence: Plan -> Design -> Coding -> Build -> Integrate -> Test -> Deploy -> Operate. It is the common grain for reasoning eligibility (1.2) and per-task gains (6.1). AgentCase decomposes it into 11 phases carrying automation and supervision per artifact.	Mapped in AgentCase
Artifact / deliverable	53 SDLC artifacts + 370 regulatory artifacts	The unit of work that carries an automation potential and a human/agent supervision split. AgentCase is artifact-oriented where the referential is SDLC-oriented, giving finer economic and supervision granularity — a strength the referential asks to reuse as an artifact catalogue annex.	AgentCase strength
Spec-Driven Development (SDD)	Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-16): SDD maturity slider	The core agentic paradigm: the specification becomes the primary, executable artifact (EARS notation) and code is a regenerable output. Opposed to Vibe Coding. x3-x10 first-try success; EDF REX -40% re-prompting. Now a first-class lever: SDD maturity cuts the verification tax (up to -50% at 100) and mitigates the AI code-quality premium. Distinct from the detailed-design artifact A012.	Mapped in AgentCase
Product status lifecycle	Product-status lifecycle weighting axis	Re-weights each phase effort by where the product sits in its lifecycle (greenfield, feature add, modernisation, maintenance, critical). Maturity and gains do not decline identically across pillars, so AgentCase lets this axis modulate per-phase effort.	AgentCase strength
V-cycle intent	Lifecycle phase tagging (V1-V7)	The engineering intent of each activity or artifact along the V-cycle (specification, design, implementation, verification, integration, validation, qualification). AgentCase tags artifacts by V-phase to drive phase-level automation and supervision.	AgentCase strength
Pilot as product	Program tab (EV-21): pilot sizing	The pilot delivers a factory (frozen harness + agents), not just an app: structured DEC -> HOW -> BLD, >=2 break-in sprints to stabilise the harness, milestones proven by demonstration not date, 3-day fail-fast iterations with full regeneration allowed. ~88% of pilots never industrialise, so distribution & governance are designed from day one. AgentCase sizes pilot duration & cost.	Mapped in AgentCase
Deployment roadmap: Discover -> Prove -> Scale	Client-view roadmap (Volet B) + Program tab (Volet A)	Decouvrir -> Prouver -> Scaler, aligned to Crawl/Walk/Run/Scale; foundations 4-12 wk, pilots 3-6 mo, scale 18+ mo. Two volets: the client deployment (B, Client-view roadmap) and the internal Capgemini capability build (A, Program tab: 50 operational -> 200 in training -> 500 targeted).	Mapped in AgentCase
3 · People & Operating Model
Pod of 2-4 (1 codebase = 1 owner)	Operating-model pod / run-team unit	The default team shape once the merge becomes the bottleneck: small pods where each developer owns one codebase (back-end, front-end, a service) so concurrent merges are limited by design. A team of 10 recomposes into ~3 pods of ~3. Requires modular, low-coupling architecture.	Mapped in AgentCase
Orchestrateur / Tech Lead	Pod Orchestrator <-> Agent Supervisor	Pilots a portfolio of agents, owns a codebase and integrates — managing agents as a lead once managed analysts. In the diamond structure (agent base, thick expert core, thin leadership), this is the pod pilot. AgentCase maps it to the Agent Supervisor role.	Mapped in AgentCase
AI Reviewer / Quality	AI Reviewer <-> AI QA	Critical review of generated code and quality gates, review-first posture; often an early-in-career profile repositioned onto judgement rather than authorship. AgentCase maps it to the AI QA agentic-supervision role.	Mapped in AgentCase
Preceptor	Preceptor (transverse role)	An explicit (no longer implicit) mentoring role: senior/junior pairing at a 3:1 to 5:1 ratio over at least 12 months, with AI configured for Socratic coaching and the junior required to explain generated code in review. Answers the knowledge-debt risk. AgentCase costs it via the HRI change model.	Mapped in AgentCase
TokenFinOps	AI FinOps role + Token economics tab	A new economic responsibility (often a hat, not a full role): steering token consumption and cost per project, spotting over-use (Opus where Sonnet suffices) and targeting training. AgentCase quantifies it in the Token economics tab and the AI FinOps role.	Mapped in AgentCase
Agentic / Dev Architect	Agent Architect <-> AI Engineer	Owns architecture (modularity so real unit tests are possible), the harness and context engineering, and designs multi-agent orchestration and guardrails. AgentCase maps it to the AI Engineer / agent-architect role.	Mapped in AgentCase
Design Authority	Design Authority (AI Office, governance instance)	Org-level governance instance that designs and validates agentic architectures, sets standards and approves autonomy raises (L3, L3->L4). Part of the tripartite governance with the AI Office and the pod Delivery Team.	Mapped in AgentCase
AI-Office	AI Office (transverse layer)	Double-faced: an internal Capgemini organisation that watches, prototypes and recommends new ADLC levers; and an external prerogative toward the client (carried by the Capgemini project lead) that steers productivity and value commitments. Anticipate a client-side Chief Agentic Officer counterpart.	Mapped in AgentCase
Context engineering (vs Prompt Engineer)	Prompt Engineer role + context-engineering skill	The core skill is less prompting than context engineering — the memory, tools and constraints that make agents effective. AgentCase keeps a Prompt Engineer role but the referential frames the higher-value skill as engineering the agent context.	AgentCase strength
AI-Native SAFe scaling	Program tab (EV-18): multi-pod overhead	Above the pod, bottlenecks move from merge to specs/validation to program/portfolio coordination. Under AI-Native SAFe, pods become augmented stream-aligned teams and enabling teams coach the human-agent collaboration patterns; coordination overhead grows ~3% per extra pod (indicative).	Mapped in AgentCase
Team topology (diamond, AI-Native SAFe)	Operating model tab + Program tab (EV-18): 10->3x3 & SAFe overhead	Teams form a diamond (agent base, thick expert core, thin leadership), not a pyramid; a team of 10 recomposes into ~3 pods of ~3. At scale, pods become augmented stream-aligned teams under AI-Native SAFe and the bottleneck moves from merge to specs/validation to program/portfolio coordination (indicative overhead ~3%/extra pod).	Mapped in AgentCase
4 · Platform & Knowledge
Living Memory / Metamodel (Knowledge Graph)	Knowledge hub CC0-CC4 (EV-10)	The Capgemini asset that captures a system architecture and logic as a metamodel / Knowledge Graph before generation, to ground agents and avoid hallucination. Proven value: quality 97% vs 31%, time -80%. AgentCase models it as the CC0-CC4 connected-context maturity driving potential, variance and KT cost.	Mapped in AgentCase
Knowledge Graph	CC "structure" sub-score of the knowledge hub	The linked, machine-readable representation of an existing system used to reconstruct specs from code for legacy/brownfield. In AgentCase it feeds the structure sub-score of the connected-context (CC) maturity.	Mapped in AgentCase
Knowledge transfer (KT Hub)	KT-cost-avoided channel of the hub	Structured onboarding and handover so newcomers ramp faster and tribal knowledge is documented. AgentCase turns hub maturity into an avoided KT/onboarding cost channel in the economics.	Mapped in AgentCase
SDD & tooling maturity	Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-16)	A 0-100 maturity from Vibe coding -> Emerging specs -> Spec-first -> Spec-Driven Development -> SDD + living metamodel. It cuts the verification tax by up to half at 100 and mitigates the AI code-quality premium, alongside a separate agent-stack/harness readiness score. The economic lever that rewards investing in specs and tooling.	Mapped in AgentCase
Agent stack (6 layers) & harness	Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-16): harness-readiness score	Six layers structure the agentic workstation: Instructions/rules (AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md), Custom subagents, Skills (SKILL.md, loaded selectively), Hooks, MCP (bridge to data/tools) and Handoff/orchestration. The harness (built collectively, frozen during validation) is now an explicit readiness score alongside SDD maturity.	Mapped in AgentCase
Four memories (context engineering)	Connected-context maturity (CC dimension)	Session, procedural, semantic and episodic memory, fed by context compression tools and above all a metamodel/Knowledge Graph. Efficiency depends as much on context as on the model. AgentCase abstracts memory maturity into the CC dimension.	Mapped in AgentCase
MCP (Model Context Protocol)	CC "agent-accessible" sub-score	The standard bridge to data, tools and systems (the "knowing"; skills are the "doing"). A gate prerequisite is an agent-accessible platform via API/MCP. Also a supply-chain risk (492 MCP servers found exposed without auth). AgentCase treats agent-accessibility as a CC sub-score.	Mapped in AgentCase
5 · Economics
Regulatory Glass Ceiling	Automation Potential x Regulatory Ceiling x Scope Weight = Effective Automation	AgentCase's most differentiating construct: compliance and criticality cap the reachable automation. The referential asks to add exactly this model to its Part 6, because without it gains look too generic or unsafe for regulated/embedded/critical work.	AgentCase strength
Effort conservation (V7)	Human FTE + Agent vFTE = Constant	A simple COMEX narrative: effort transfers from human to agent rather than headcount being simply cut. Prevents presenting AI as pure workforce reduction. Not formalised in the referential — AgentCase contributes it to Part 6.	AgentCase strength
Virtual FTE (vFTE)	Agent vFTE	Human effort replaced by agents, expressed in FTE-equivalent so the mixed team can be reasoned about economically. Referential ratio example: a team of 10 -> 6 -> 0 agents at constant deliverable = -50% time. AgentCase computes vFTE per artifact and phase.	AgentCase strength
Adoption ramp (maturity realisation)	Three-point annual adoption ramp (Y1/Y2/Y3), constant within year	Progressive activation of the automation potential over time; maturity acts as a realisation factor (Abaque B) deciding what fraction of the addressable gain is actually captured. METR: experienced devs were -19% slower early 2025, then ~neutral in 2026 after re-learning.	Aligned
Token cache & total-cost	Program tab (EV-28) + Token economics	The goal is the lowest TOTAL cost (tokens + human review), not the lowest token bill: a better model at 5x token price can cut rework enough to land near ~$30/dev-day. Prompt caching removes up to ~90% of repeated input cost. AgentCase surfaces the cache effect and the total-cost logic.	Mapped in AgentCase
Abaques A & B (addressable vs net gain)	Eligibility & Abaques tab (EV-27): per-phase A x B - tax = net	Now a first-class reconciliation: Abaque A (addressable per phase e.g. Coding 30-60%, Test 30-70%) times Abaque B (maturity capture: L1 0-15%, L2 30-50%, L3 50-75%, L4 75-100%), optionally times the pillar leverage, MINUS the verification tax = net gain per phase. Explains the ~10% net productivity paradox. The tax can be fed into the economics via a toggle.	Mapped in AgentCase
ROI & payback	Financial tab (ROI, NPV, payback, break-even)	The deterministic economics of the case. Because COCOMO/KLOC breaks down when AI writes much of the code, AgentCase estimates from spec x capacity x supervision x tokens rather than lines of code.	AgentCase strength
FinOps GenAI (token economics)	Token economics tab + AI FinOps	Cost shifts from man-time to tokens + supervision: ~13$/active dev-day, rising to 500-2000$/month if uncontrolled; token cache cuts input -90%. The rule is not the cheapest model but the lowest TOTAL cost (tokens + human): best models cut rework, so realistic cost is ~30$/dev-day. AgentCase models this in Token economics.	AgentCase strength
Uncertainty & confidence	Monte Carlo + Decision Intelligence	A probabilistic verdict rather than a single point, matching the referential T0/T1/T2 logic that gains are only engaged once measured in ramp-up. AgentCase runs Monte Carlo and issues a confidence-conditional verdict.	AgentCase strength
6 · Commercial
Effort & Tools	Commercial model — ET (EV-07)	Bill human effort at the rate card and sell the agent cost on top. Simple and transparent, but 100% of productivity gains are handed to the client and billing the agent cost is tedious. AgentCase prices it in the Commercial model tab.	Mapped in AgentCase
AI-augmented humans	Commercial model — AAH	Total price includes human + agents, raising the blended day-rate. Variant 2a sells token cost as a TJM uplift (fits client-exclusive/infra-client); 2b simulates agent effort in man-days. Over-sell is mandatory and the unpredictable agent cost is carried by Capgemini. AgentCase shows the break-even uplift.	Mapped in AgentCase
Mixed teams / agentshore	Commercial model — AGS (capacity, T0/T1/T2 capacity tiers)	The client grid shows a human AND agent rate card for a very attractive blended rate; humans and agents are sold inextricably so the client cannot use the agent rate alone. Illustrative doctrine assumptions ~800 EUR/day human, ~133 EUR/day agent. AgentCase prices capacity with T0/T1/T2 capacity tiers by AI authorization.	Mapped in AgentCase
T0 / T1 / T2 commitment calendar	Roadmap gates: Validate (measure) -> Pilot & Prove -> Decide & Scale	The mechanism to commit at fixed-price before real productivity is known: T0 engagement start (gains estimated by the calculator), T1 ramp-up (gains measured), T2 commitment effective (gains engaged only once measured, then tracked). Measurement precedes commitment. AgentCase maps it to the roadmap gates. (Distinct from Agentshore capacity tiers.)	Mapped in AgentCase
Gain sharing / outcome-based	Commercial model — GS	Base discount plus a share of realised savings. The referential v1 deliberately keeps outcome-based and managed-services OUT of scope (it focuses on the three TJM models), while the market moves toward outcome-based and hybrid pricing. AgentCase implements GS as an available model where control allows (MSC).	Referential only
Pricing logic	Commercial model client-vs-margin views	How value is priced and split across the three TJM models, chosen by client expectations and constraints; who pays the agent cost (client vs Capgemini) depends on the model. AgentCase shows client TCO against Capgemini margin side by side.	Mapped in AgentCase
7 · Transformation & Change
Change management (people first)	Change Cost Model (EV-08)	Most AI deployments fail for human, not technical, reasons: the tool is imposed while triggering job fear. ~40% of staff fear losing their job to AI, up to 89% are concerned. The referential is explicit that the human side is the hard part. AgentCase costs and paces it via the change model.	Mapped in AgentCase
Upskilling (90/10 shift)	HRI "skills & training" dimension	Kent Beck: ~90% of traditional coding skills are devalued, ~10% (vision, planning, specification, verification) are decupled; code is only ~20% of SDLC work. Core skills become agent orchestration, review judgement, system design and context engineering. AgentCase scores readiness on this dimension.	Mapped in AgentCase
Champions network	HRI champions dimension + community of practice	A peer champion network relays adoption, per-population "what I gain" messaging and role-based training, sustained by communities of practice as coaching is weaned off. AgentCase scores champion coverage in the readiness index.	Mapped in AgentCase
Coaching curve & autonomy weaning	Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-19)	Coaching is not a lump: it starts at ~40% of capacity in Y1 and decays to ~20% at cruise as support is weaned through four tiers - intensive coaching (preceptor 3:1-5:1) -> on-demand support -> self-service (playbooks, internal certification) -> community of practice. Deliberate support decay avoids permanent dependency.	Mapped in AgentCase
Preceptorship (knowledge debt)	HRI + Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-19): coaching curve	The deliberate answer to knowledge debt (Addy Osmani): supervising without practising yields shallow understanding. Senior/junior binomes 3:1-5:1, Socratic coaching, review-first, protected mentoring time. Now modelled as a declining coaching-load curve (~40% of capacity Y1 -> ~20% at cruise) with the four autonomy-weaning tiers.	Mapped in AgentCase
Human readiness	Human Readiness Index (EV-08)	The people-side readiness of the transformation (leadership, fears, skills, champions, preceptorship, rituals), scored 0-100. Low readiness slows adoption and must not be over-sold. AgentCase turns it into an adoption drag on the S-curve and a change cost.	Mapped in AgentCase
Adoption & fears (perceived vs real)	HRI "fears & resistance" dimension + adoption drag	The productivity feeling often exceeds real gains: DORA shows each +25% AI adoption came with -1.5% delivery speed and -7.2% stability; Google saw 74% generated code for only ~15% real gain. Steer by measurement, not perception. AgentCase reflects this as adoption drag and Monte Carlo variance.	Mapped in AgentCase
8 · Governance, Safety & Compliance
Human-in-the-Lead	Human supervision floor + posture (EV-03)	The human is fully responsible not as a line-by-line reviewer but as the designer of the agentic system (specs, harness, gates, agents) that produced the code; a defect in output means that system is not yet right. Reinforced human-in-the-lead for critical systems. AgentCase quantifies the irreducible supervision floor.	Mapped in AgentCase
Human-on / over-the-loop	Supervision posture (30-60% / < 30%)	Graduated human-control levels below full lead, set by risk-based review effort as maturity rises. AgentCase derives the posture per phase from the floor: >=60% in-the-lead, 30-60% on-the-loop, <30% over-the-loop.	Mapped in AgentCase
Review gates by phase (Human-in-the-Lead)	Artifact supervision % (human split)	Mandatory human review gates by phase (Ideate -> Design -> Build -> Test -> Deploy -> Operate), plus quality control C2 (tests, mutation testing, security) and an enlarged Definition of Done. The CI is the wall that does not move: gates are mandatory and non-bypassable before merge. AgentCase quantifies the split per artifact.	Mapped in AgentCase
Human responsibility (accountability)	Supervision floor + AI authority cap	The agent is never responsible; accountability stays human, with the corollary that the human must be given the tools to prevent error. Multi-agent validation (code / critique / test / conformity) distributes detection, not responsibility. AgentCase caps AI authority and enforces the floor.	Aligned
Certification-bound software	Critical & Regulated report (EV-11)	Delivery under safety certification (DO-178, IEC 61508, ISO 26262, IEC 62304): prerequisites are harder or inapplicable (hardware-in-the-loop, air-gap, safety certification), lowering the engageable maturity. AgentCase exports a dedicated Critical & Regulated report.	Mapped in AgentCase
Embedded / critical / sovereign	Regulatory catalog + embedded chapter	The Software Engineering differentiator vs APPS: embedded/firmware (hardware, real-time), critical systems (safety, reinforced human-in-the-lead) and sovereign (sovereign infra/models, air-gap) harden prerequisites and lower reachable maturity. AgentCase carries the regulatory catalogue and embedded scope.	Aligned
DO-178C / ISO 26262 / IEC 61508 / IEC 62304	Regulatory norms catalog (47 norm sets, 370 artifacts)	Sector safety and assurance standards that a critical or embedded product must satisfy — potentially shifting the delivered product into the EU AI Act high-risk tier. AgentCase maps them to 47 norm sets and 370 regulatory artifacts with per-phase ceilings.	Aligned
Responsible AI (three frameworks)	Risk & Compliance tab (EV-25) + regulatory artifacts	A complementary, non-substitutable trio: EU AI Act (legal; unacceptable/high/limited/minimal tiers, conformity assessments for high-risk), NIST AI RMF (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage) and ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system). Now operationalised by the AI-Act tier classifier, which raises obligations and the Decision Intelligence bar per mission.	Aligned
EU AI-Act risk tiers	Risk & Compliance tab (EV-25): tier selector + obligations	The delivered product is classified per mission: unacceptable (prohibited), high-risk (Annex III: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, human oversight, conformity assessment, EU database registration), limited (transparency) or minimal. High and unacceptable tiers raise obligations and the Decision Intelligence bar.	Mapped in AgentCase
Agentic risk register	Risk & Compliance tab (EV-17): 8 risks, prob x impact, mitigation	A register of the agentic-specific risks with probability/impact/mitigation: prompt & indirect injection (~73% of deployments vulnerable), MCP/skills supply chain (492 exposed servers, 1184 malicious skills), secret theft, model/vendor dependency, hallucination/false success, knowledge debt, plus the ~88% pilot-mortality and ~40% cancellation macro-risks. The consolidated score can raise the Decide & Scale verdict bar.	Mapped in AgentCase
AI-BOM & enlarged Definition of Done	Maturity & Measurement tab (EV-24) + Critical & Regulated report	The CI is the wall that does not move: non-bypassable gates, C2 quality control (tests, mutation testing, security), anti-false-success guardrails, an enlarged Definition of Done (agent provenance, review evidence, security check, doc, AI-BOM entry) and an AI Bill of Materials listing the models, datasets, MCP services and skills used for traceability and supply-chain audit.	Mapped in AgentCase
AI code-quality risk premium	Risk & Compliance tab (EV-23): premium on tax + DI bar	A tunable premium capturing the higher defect density of AI-written code (correctness x1.75, maintainability x1.64, security x1.57). When applied it multiplies the verification tax and adds a stability term to the Decision Intelligence bar; it is mitigated by SDD maturity. Answers the reference caution that heavy AI adoption can degrade stability (DORA -7.2%).	AgentCase strength
Confidentiality & sovereignty	Program tab (EV-26): IP/confidentiality/sovereignty per context	Never send confidential context to an unmastered model; apply GDPR and partition by engagement context. Sovereignty = control of model & data hosting via SovBox (Mistral on-prem) or air-gap. Now a per-context synthesis (MSC/MSI/STA) of IP ownership of deliverables & assets, confidentiality posture and sovereignty, exported in the Critical & Regulated report.	Aligned
9 · KPI & Performance
Human early-warning indicators	Maturity & Measurement socle (EV-20)	Leading indicators of skill erosion the reference insists on: time-to-judgment (how long to reach a sound decision - rising is a warning), bus factor (concentration of irreplaceable knowledge) and % of mentoring time compensated (is coaching protected against billable pressure). Added to the measurement socle with target bands and a human-readiness flag.	Mapped in AgentCase
SPACE framework	Maturity & Measurement socle (EV-29)	Beyond throughput: Satisfaction & well-being, Performance (outcomes), Activity, Communication & collaboration, Efficiency & flow. Paired with DORA, AI signals and human indicators so an inflated output volume cannot hide slower or less stable delivery. Steer value, not lines produced.	Mapped in AgentCase
DORA metrics	KPI Scorecard + Maturity & Measurement socle (EV-29)	Delivery flow and stability: deploy frequency, lead time, change failure rate, MTTR. DORA alone is no longer enough in 2026 (adoption can lift throughput while degrading stability -7.2%), so it is now one of four families in the measurement socle: DORA + SPACE + AI signals + human indicators, with change-failure-rate as the stability guardrail.	Mapped in AgentCase
Quality gates (AI defects)	Risk & Compliance tab (EV-23) + Quality view	AI code shows correctness x1.75, maintainability x1.64, security x1.57 issues vs human; >=62% of generated programs carry a known weakness. The AI code-quality risk premium raises the verification tax and the DI bar (mitigated by SDD maturity). Anti-false-success guardrails watch agents that game the green (deleted tests, disabled lint).	AgentCase strength
Cycle time / lead time	Velocity tab	The flow speed of delivery, segmented by AI involvement so an inflated output volume does not hide slower or less stable delivery. AgentCase reports velocity across the horizon.	AgentCase strength
Rework / verification tax	Eligibility & Abaques tab (EV-27): computed deduction	The verification tax is now COMPUTED (rate = editable base x average human supervision, capped 60%) and, when the toggle is on, deducted from yearly savings so net gain is explicit. Together with code churn and escaped defects it is the review effort that turns addressable into net gain and must never be neglected.	AgentCase strength
Business value metrics (beyond billable staffing)	KPI cascade (N0 financial -> N4 operational)	Steer value, not only cost: an architect who builds an agent solving 40% of a volume forever creates value even if the timesheet says otherwise, so the referential asks to move beyond billable-staffing rate as the sole performance metric. AgentCase cascades N0 financial to N4 operational KPIs.	Mapped in AgentCase
