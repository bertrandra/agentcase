🎯 Scope — target artifact selection
Select the individual artifacts (grouped by phase) included in the scenario. By default, all are included. Tick a phase header to toggle all its artifacts, or pick a few target artifacts. The input team is treated as dedicated to the selected scope — so automation depth is driven by the selected artifacts' automatable average (the Target automation potential auto-fits to it, editable on the Parameters tab), not diluted by the scope's share of the whole lifecycle. Selection cascades across every tab — team reduction, costs, vFTE, ROI, KPI, compliance and the performance (productivity, velocity, quality).
