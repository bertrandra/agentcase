Prices supervision as a decision, not a wall. Provisioned (default) solves for the supervisor headcount your target agent capacity needs, prices it, and never caps throughput — this is the app's pre-existing run-team sizing policy, now named and surfaced. Constrained fixes headcount and caps achievable capacity at what that team can absorb — the "what today's team can absorb without hiring" sensitivity. Plus a DORA read-out pulled forward from P5.
